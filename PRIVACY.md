# Privacy Policy

This is a personal automation tool used by a single person (the developer) for learning and demonstration purposes.

## What it accesses
The app connects to the developer's own Google account (Google Sheets and Gmail) so that automated workflows can read and write the developer's own spreadsheets and send emails.

## Data collected
This app does not collect, store, sell, or share data about any other person. No data is sent to third parties other than the Google services the developer chooses to connect.

## Data storage
Any data handled by the workflows stays in the developer's own Google account and on the developer's own computer.

## Contact
For questions, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
